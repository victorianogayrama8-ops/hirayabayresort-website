Helpful Tips

Frequently Asked by Prospective Guests

Is outside food allowed, or do you charge corkage? expand_more

Hiraya Bay Resort proudly maintains a Zero Corkage Policy for all standard food, grilling items, and snacks. You may bring your own marinated meats for our beachfront BBQ grills! Minimal disposal fees only apply to large electric appliances.

What are the operating hours for the mountain-view pool? expand_more

The infinity-edge pool is open daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM for overnight guests. Day tour swimming runs from 8:00 AM until 5:00 PM to allow for evening campfire tranquility.

Can day-tour visitors join the evening bonfire circle? expand_more

Yes! Day visitors who book our Sunset Campfire extension (5:00 PM - 9:00 PM) are welcome to gather around the fire pit, roast marshmallows, and enjoy music under the stars.
