Unplug • Restore • Breathe

Where emerald forest canopies kiss the calm sea waters

Hiraya Bay Resort invites you to celebrate raw island tranquility. Nestled between gentle mountain ridges and the ocean horizon, our retreat celebrates traditional Filipino hospitality through handcrafted bamboo kubo pavilions, cliffside swimming pools, and open-air seaside verandas.

From cooling morning swims beneath breezy almond trees to crackling campfires where travelers share tales under untamed constellations, every hour here slows down to the cadence of the tides.
