Good to Know

Resort Guidelines & Policies

Everything you need to plan a tranquil, hassle-free escape at Hiraya Bay Resort.

A 50% reservation deposit via GCash or Bank Transfer is requested to secure Kubo cabanas and overnight cottages. Deposits are 100% rebookable or refundable if notified at least 5 days prior to your scheduled stay. In cases of gale warnings or bad weather declared by PAGASA, complimentary reschedule credits are granted.
Day Tour cottage rentals run from 8:00 AM through 5:00 PM. For Overnight Cabana guests, official check-in begins at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 NN the following day. Early arrivals may relax in the common garden lounge area if cabana turnover is underway.
Yes! We are proud to be pet-friendly. Up to two well-behaved fur babies per group are welcome in the outdoor grounds, Kubo dining huts, and picnic lawns. For sanitary standards, pets are not allowed inside the swimming pool waters. Please bring leashes and personal pet waste bags.
Proper swimwear (rashguards, boardshorts, swimsuits, or nylon shirts) is required when dipping in the infinity pool. Cotton shirts and pants with zippers are discouraged for water filtration hygiene. Sanctuary quiet hours commence promptly at 10:00 PM to respect resting guests and nature stillness.
