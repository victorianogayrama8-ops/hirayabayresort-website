By Private Vehicle

Fastest & Scenic Drive

  • check_circle Take the Southern Coastal Expressway and exit towards the Provincial Western Bay Highway.
  • check_circle Pass the landmark bay bridge and follow signs pointing to "Sitio Baybay Coastal Road".
  • check_circle Free covered and secured guest parking with 24/7 attendant directly behind the bamboo entrance.
directions_bus

By Public Commute

Bus & Tricycle Route

  • check_circle Board any south-bound provincial bus line with express service to Town Proper Central Terminal.
  • check_circle From the terminal, take a local seaside tricycle directly to "Hiraya Bay Resort Entrance" (approx. 15 mins).
  • check_circle Standard local fare is approx. ₱150 - ₱200 per special chartered tricycle trip for up to 4 persons.

Guest Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers regarding walk-ins, food corkage, pet policies, and amenities at Hiraya Bay.

Walk-in guests are welcome during weekdays subject to daily capacity limits. However, on Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays, we strictly prioritize confirmed bookings to avoid overcrowding and ensure every guest has an assigned cottage and comfortable pool access.
Yes! Guests are welcome to bring cooked food and packed meals with zero corkage fee. We also offer dedicated grilling stations on a first-come, shared basis. Moderate alcoholic drinks have no fee; commercial-volume catering or external electrical appliances (like electric cookers) may require prior coordination with our front desk.
Hiraya Bay is pet-friendly! Fur parents can bring small-to-medium well-trained dogs and cats on leashes in all open-air ground areas and seaside cottages. For hygiene and safety standards, pets are not permitted inside the freshwater pool waters.
Both major national cellular carriers (Globe Telecom and Smart Communications) provide dependable 4G LTE signal across the property. In addition, complimentary high-speed Starlink WiFi is accessible around the pool area, main pavilion, and front desk office.
We strongly recommend reef-safe sunscreen, aqua shoes for beach walks along the shoreline, swimwear, personal towels, and an evening jacket or hoodie if staying for the sunset bonfire. If bringing fresh grilling ingredients, charcoal is available for purchase at our tuck shop.
